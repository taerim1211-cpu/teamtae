College admissions can feel like a guessing game. In Michigan, a 3.0 GPA takes a lot of the guessing out. Under the **Michigan Assured Admission Pact (MAAP)**, 11 of the state's 15 public universities guarantee admission to Michigan high school graduates with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher.

## How it works

- **The requirement:** a cumulative high school GPA of 3.0 or above.
- **You still have to apply.** The guarantee only works if you submit an application to each school by its deadline.
- **Tests are optional.** All 15 Michigan public universities are test-optional.
- **It only covers the fall right after you graduate from high school.**

## The 11 schools in the pact

| University | Average GPA of first-year students |
|---|---|
| U-M Dearborn | 3.70 |
| Saginaw Valley State | 3.55 |
| U-M Flint | 3.54 |
| Oakland | 3.50 |
| Central Michigan | 3.48 |
| Wayne State | 3.47 |
| Grand Valley State | 3.41 |
| Northern Michigan | 3.36 |
| Eastern Michigan | 3.35 |
| Ferris State | 3.35 |
| Lake Superior State | 3.21 |

Many of these schools enroll students with average GPAs well above 3.0. So for a student with a 3.0 or 3.1, the pact makes a real difference: it turns a school that might have been a reach into a sure thing.

## The 4 schools that aren't included

The state's most selective public universities aren't part of the pact:

- **University of Michigan (Ann Arbor):** first-year average GPA of 3.92
- **Michigan Tech:** 3.84
- **Michigan State:** 3.72
- **Western Michigan:** 3.55

U-M, Michigan State, and Michigan Tech all have **early action deadlines on November 1**. Western Michigan's early action deadline is December 1.

## What the guarantee doesn't cover

- **Specific majors.** The pact guarantees admission to the university, not to competitive programs like nursing or engineering, which can have extra requirements.
- **Scholarships.** Getting in is guaranteed, but scholarship money isn't.
- **Disciplinary history.** Schools that review discipline or criminal records still do.

## Watch the scholarship deadlines

This is the most important part. The pact schools use **rolling admission**, which means they accept applications over many months and you could apply as late as summer. But their scholarship deadlines come much sooner:

- **Northern Michigan:** Presidential Scholars Competition dates in late October and **November 2**
- **Central Michigan:** Centralis Honors Scholarship, **November 2**
- **Saginaw Valley State:** President's Scholarship, **December 1**
- **Wayne State:** priority deadline **December 1**
- **Grand Valley State and U-M Flint:** merit scholarships, **March 1**

If you wait until spring to apply, you'll still get in, but you may miss out on thousands of dollars.

## Key takeaways

- **A 3.0 guarantees you a spot** at 11 Michigan public universities, as long as you apply on time.
- **U-M, Michigan State, Michigan Tech, and Western Michigan** aren't part of it and have their own deadlines.
- **Your major isn't guaranteed.** Check the requirements for competitive programs.
- **Apply early for scholarships.** Several scholarship deadlines fall in early November and December.
